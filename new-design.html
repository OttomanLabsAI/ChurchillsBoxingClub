Our Gym

Finally — a boxing gym culture where everyone is welcome.

At Churchill's, being a boxer means more than throwing punches. It's discipline, determination, and belonging to a supportive community. No matter your background, fitness level, or experience, you'll find a home here. We pride ourselves on a culture where respect and encouragement sit at the heart of everything we do.

We offer a full range of group classes — from light combat and sparring for beginners to simulated ring fights for advanced fighters. Prefer to go solo? Our one-to-one programs come tailored to your goals, complete with a personalised nutrition plan.
