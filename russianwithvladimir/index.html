Ме́тод · the method

Apps teach you to match words.
I teach you to speak.

Most learners get stuck the same way: years of flashcards, no accountability, and total silence the moment a real Russian speaker appears. The Accelerator flips the order — you speak from the start, and the grammar is built to serve the conversation.

РАЗ / one

Start where you are

Complete beginner? Perfect. We begin with the alphabet, sounds, and your first working phrases — several of my students held real conversations within months of learning their first letter.

ДВА / two

Build the machine

Verb conjugations, adjectives, sentence patterns, and core vocabulary — food, places, work, daily life. Structured, gradual, and paced for busy adults with jobs and families.

ТРИ / three

Say what you actually think

We move into opinions, psychology, politics — the topics real relationships are made of. This is where conversations with a spouse or friend stop being small talk and start being meaningful.

Слу́шай · listen

The car and the man

When my German student Claudia started, she heard мужчи́на (a man) every time I said маши́на (a car). Seven months later she was following Russian radio programs about psychology and procrastination. Listening isn't a talent — it's trained, one confused pair of words at a time.

You learn to speak by speaking. So we speak.

Вопро́сы · questions

Before you ask

I don't know a single Russian word. Can I really start?
Yes — and you're in the majority. Many students begin at the alphabet. Dana, Claudia, and Renate all started from absolute zero and were holding conversations within months.
I'm genuinely busy. Will this fit my life?
The program is built for exactly that. Dana is a working nurse and a mother of two. The pace is structured and gradual — designed so consistent small sessions beat heroic weekend cramming.
What happens in the free training?
Vladimir walks through the method behind the Accelerator — how students go from zero to real conversation, what the coaching looks like, and how to know if it's right for you. Watch it first, then decide.
How long until I can hold a real conversation?
Students in the program report first real conversations within about 5–7 months — from basics at month one to deeper topics like opinions and psychology later on. Your pace depends on the time you put in.
